### Imagine this:
A world without artificial intelligence—where people use em dashes without thinking twice—where people delve into things without fearing that that word alone would make people believe it’s AI, and where developers type every line manually with their primary source of help being Stack Overflow and YouTube videos. That was me, all the way back in 2023, searching up "how to change the text of an element in js." No GitHub Copilot, no Microsoft Copilot, no ChatGPT, no Claude, none of it.

Every project I completed, even if it was a days between dates calculator I had no idea how to make that 1 000 people had already made, I felt accomplished for putting in the effort. It brought me joy to see working versions of my code that I probably used Google 30 times for. No AI overview, by the way. Nowadays, you can type a simple "make me a days between dates calculator web app" prompt into Claude and it spits out the same thing, but in 20 seconds instead of 3 days. It disheartens me how much AI is used entirely instead of making the same thing at least half yourself.

So, will you escape the AI trap and stop vibecoding every idea you have just so it "gets finished (quicker)" or because you’re "too lazy?" Or will you use what you’ve learned to make your great ideas come true with the ability to proudly exclaim: "I made it myself!!!"

Your choice.
